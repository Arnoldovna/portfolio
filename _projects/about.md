description: 'I’m Marina Arnoldovna Wilgelm, a Berlin-based frontend developer with over six years of experience and a master’s degree in design. My career began in textile design, including work for <a href="https://www.lalaberlin.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Lala Berlin</a>, before I found my way into software development. This combination of design and technology continues to shape how I approach my work today. <br><br>
My experience spans both agency work and product development. At <a href="https://www.edenspiekermann.com/eu/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Edenspiekermann</a> and <a href="https://pooliestudios.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Pooliestudios</a>, I worked on websites and digital platforms for clients including Bündnis 90/Die Grünen, Schrot&amp;Korn, BioHandel, JUVE and azur, with a focus on frontend development, design systems and CMS-based solutions. <br><br>
Today, I work at <a href="https://www.datatrain.de/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Datatrain</a>, developing white-label products and dashboards with Angular and TypeScript for the housing industry, including projects for Gewobag and Gesobau. <br><br>
AI-assisted development has become an essential part of my workflow. Alongside daily coding with Claude Code, I’ve helped develop AI tooling at Datatrain that makes it easier for frontend teams to work with AI coding agents. In 2026, I completed the <a href="https://dbuas.de/ki-weiterbildung/agentic-ai-designer-operations/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Agentic AI Designer (DBU)</a> programme, exploring AI assistants and workflow automation. <br><br>
A generalist at heart, I enjoy exploring new technologies, connecting different disciplines and turning ideas into working applications. <br><br>
Outside of work, I love creating things inspired by colour, craftsmanship and my background in design.'
